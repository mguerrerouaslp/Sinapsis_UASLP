# Contributors & Credits — Sinapsis

The following individuals and institutions are the authors, sponsors, and contributors behind the development of the **Sinapsis** typeface (Regular):

---

## Primary Credits
* **Juan Fernando Cárdenas Guillén** — Original handwriting and conceptual inspiration.
* **Manuel Guerrero Salinas (Bluetypo)** — Digital type design, digitization, vectorization, and digital font production.

---

## Academic Institution & Workshop
* **Taller de Arquitectura Sinapsis** — Facultad del Hábitat, Universidad Autónoma de San Luis Potosí (UASLP). Founded in 2011 by architect Juan Fernando Cárdenas Guillén.
* **Bluetypo** ([bluetypo.com](https://bluetypo.com)) — Type engineering supervision, vector contour optimization, OpenType scripting, and quality assurance.
