The Sinapsis font software is an Open Access educational, architectural, and design resource developed to advance visual culture within the Sinapsis Architecture Workshop, the Facultad del Hábitat (UASLP), and the international creative community. Sinapsis is conceived and released exclusively as a single, authentic Regular style faithful to the original architectural handwriting of Arq. Juan Fernando Cárdenas Guillén.
